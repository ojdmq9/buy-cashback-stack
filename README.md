## The fine print that decides whether you actually get paid

This is where deal articles usually go quiet, and it's the most useful part of the whole process. TopCashback's Best Buy terms include several rules that can quietly void your cash back:

1. **Start with an empty cart.** If items are already in your Best Buy cart when you click through from TopCashback, the purchase may not be eligible. This is the single most common tracking mistake.
2. **Cash back applies to item prices only** — taxes, shipping and fees are excluded from the calculation.
3. **No reselling or bulk buying.** Purchases made with the intent to resell, or bulk orders, can be declined.
4. **You have 30 days to claim a missing transaction.** If your order doesn't track automatically, you need to submit a Missing Cash Back Claim within 30 days. After that, the money is gone.
5. **Initial tracking amounts can be lower than the final amount.** The transaction may first show a smaller figure and correct itself once Best Buy approves it — don't panic at the pending number.
6. **Don't use Safari.** TopCashback states it can't guarantee tracking on Safari and recommends Chrome or another browser.
7. **Complete the purchase in the same session.** Jumping to a coupon site, switching devices, or letting the cart sit overnight can break the tracking cookie.

One genuinely reassuring detail: the rate you see when you activate cash back is the rate locked to that purchase, even if the published rate drops later while your order is still processing.
